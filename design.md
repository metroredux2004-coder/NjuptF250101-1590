# Design — Elastic-Sphere Gas Lab
- Aesthetic: dark lab-instrument / oscilloscope. Background #0b0d10 with faint grid; panels #12161b, borders #222a33.
- Accents: Sim A cyan #3ad6ff, Sim B amber #ffb02e; reference curves (ideal/Maxwell) white 60%.
- Particles colored by speed: deep blue -> cyan -> yellow -> red.
- Type: IBM Plex Sans (UI), IBM Plex Mono (numbers, labels). Small caps-style uppercase micro labels, tracking 0.12em.
- Layout: two side-by-side simulation panels (A/B) for direct comparison; presets bar on top; charts in 2x2 under each canvas.
